# OverTheWire Narnia Writeup

Hello! These are my writeups for the [Narnia](https://overthewire.org/wargames/narnia/) wargame hosted by OverTheWire. The theme of the wargame is around beginner binary exploitation, with each challenge being a new flavour of binexp, or a more difficult version of a previous challenge. Overall these challenges were pretty fun and I hope my writeup makes sense. 

> I include the flags for each level in the writeup so that if you just want to give a particular level a try you can - hence why I don't provide the flag to the last 'level'
>> Flags also get randomised every now and again anyways so no guarentees that the flags will be the same when you try them
